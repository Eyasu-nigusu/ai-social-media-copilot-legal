Data deletion

How to disconnect Instagram and request removal of information handled by the current MVP.

Last updated October 24, 2026

Disconnect the Instagram account

  1. Open Instagram and go to Settings and activity.
  2. Find Website permissions, then open Apps and websites. Menu names can vary by app version.
  3. Find AI Social Media Copilot in the active apps list and remove its access.

Removing access prevents the app from continuing to use that authorization, subject to Meta’s handling of revocation. You can also stop using the dashboard.

Request deletion from the app operator

The current MVP has no database and no automated deletion workflow or in-app request form. To request removal of the server-side Instagram access token or ask the operator to check for information associated with your use, contact the person or operator who gave you access to the app.

  1. State that you are requesting deletion or removal of your Instagram connection.
  2. Include your Instagram username so the operator can identify the connection.
  3. Do not send your Instagram password, access token, or other credentials.

The operator must remove the token from the server environment manually. This page does not claim that such removal is automatic. Since the MVP does not use a database for Instagram data, there is no app database record to delete; temporary runtime or hosting logs may depend on the environment used to run the app.

Where to send the request

This project does not currently list a dedicated support email or legal entity. Use the same contact channel through which you received access to the app. If you are running a local copy yourself, remove the INSTAGRAM_ACCESS_TOKEN value from your local server environment and restart the app.

This page describes the current MVP. The product may change as it develops; material changes to this page will be reflected here.
